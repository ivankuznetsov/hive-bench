# Resume terminal review failures in place

**Problem:** Several OpenCode cells completed execute and open-pr, then failed a
later review phase during local route preflight. The driver could resume only
stage 4, so retrying discarded an identity-verified candidate and rebought the
entire model run.

**Action:** The driver now recognizes a provenance-matched terminal review
failure with a retained execute patch and restarts stage 6 in place. The stage
script skips plan, execute, and open-pr; preserves the original execute patch;
and lets Hive continue its durable review phase/pass state. Auth and usage-limit
failures remain excluded.

**Evidence:** `test/hive_driver_test.rb` pins in-place review recovery,
telemetry, candidate preservation, exact resume environment, and rejection for
missing patches, auth/limits, and identity drift.
