## Headline findings

Test split. "Gold" is the 392 test cases (499 across both splits) where Claude's adjudication and a blind second annotator, Gemini 4 Neon, gave the same label. On every gold case the incumbent is a regex or heuristic. Read the incumbent column with care: the adjudicated sample was drawn toward suspected incumbent misfires, so it shows how the incumbent does on hard cases, not on everyday traffic.

- **The decision models beat the regex/heuristic incumbent on all 8 points that have gold.** Pooled accuracy: incumbent 0.648, OpenAI direct 0.926, Jev direct 0.941. Paired, each direct model is right where the incumbent is wrong on 124 cases, and wrong where it is right on 15 (OpenAI) and 9 (Jev). The largest gaps are visual_capture_need (path regex 0.476, models 0.968 / 0.984), plan_review_level (regex 0.548, both models 0.903), patrol_finding_dedup (token overlap 0.687, models 0.970 / 1.000) and ci_failure_triage (0.605, models 0.954 / 0.837). The smallest is recovery_same_root (fingerprint 0.894, models 0.979 / 0.957).
- **They do not beat Hive's LLM steps, and no one predicts the operator.**
  - review_finding_triage: Hive's triage agent agrees with the outcome label 0.980; the models score 0.675–0.824. The outcome is partly downstream of the agent's own label (a fix commit follows *because* triage chose auto-fix), so the incumbent has a head start. Still, nothing here argues for replacing it.
  - plan_review_decision_triage: incumbent 0.459, models 0.456–0.481 against the operator's later decision (κ ≈ 0).
  - patrol_fix_semantic_admission has no gold. The models agree with Hive's LLM on only 0.461–0.748 of cases.
- **Direct beats decomposed.** Pooled gold: OpenAI 0.926 vs 0.842, Jev 0.941 vs 0.847. Paired, direct wins 56–23 for OpenAI and 43–6 for Jev. Decomposed loses most where its rule abstains or fails closed: task_workflow_routing (0.538 / 0.346, the one model score below that point's constant-`full` incumbent at 0.423), visual_capture_need (OpenAI 0.619) and ci_failure_triage (0.698 / 0.767). It wins only for OpenAI, on plan_review_level (1.000 vs 0.903), provider_failure_class (0.969 vs 0.891) and recovery_same_root (1.000 vs 0.979). plan_review_level's adjudicated level was itself derived through the same `rule.py`, which favours the decomposed variant there.
- **OpenAI vs Jev: accuracy is a tie.** Direct, pooled gold: 0.926 vs 0.941. Paired, only OpenAI is right on 13 cases and only Jev on 19, which is within noise at n = 392. Per point, Jev direct leads on refactor_merge_classifier (0.941 vs 0.863) and provider_failure_class (0.953 vs 0.891); OpenAI direct leads on ci_failure_triage (0.954 vs 0.837).
- **Jev is half the price and has a tighter tail.** Median latency is close: direct p50 168 ms for OpenAI, 183 ms for Jev. At p95 it is 401 ms vs 282 ms (decomposed: 491 vs 289). At list price, 1,000 direct rows cost $0.43 on OpenAI and $0.21 on Jev. The whole run billed $4.22 and $2.14.
- **Reliability.** No row ended in an error on either provider. Jev needed its long-context header for 168 patrol_fix_semantic_admission rows whose state exceeds its 32k-token per-question limit. OpenAI rejects a choice question with a single option (2 rows), so the runner answers that question locally as forced.
- **The gold set is only as firm as the annotators' agreement.** Claude and Neon agree on 88.6% of the 563 adjudicated rows (mean κ 0.79). The weakest points are task_workflow_routing (60.3%, κ 0.39) and ci_failure_triage (75.6%, κ 0.66). The 64 disagreements await the owner's tie-break and are not in gold. Both annotators are LLMs, like the systems under test, so blind spots they share would flatter the models against the regex.

## How to read the per-point tables

`acc / κ (n)` is accuracy, Cohen's kappa and row count. *Decisive A/B*: coverage (share of answered rows that are decisive), then accuracy vs system and vs the point's decisive gold (`g`: adjudicated if the point has it, else outcome). Direct: A = confidence ≥ 0.8 (choice) or noul ≤ 0.3/≥ 0.7; B = confidence ≥ 0.9 or noul ≤ 0.1/≥ 0.9. Decomposed: A = rule settled (no abstention); B = settled and every component answer decisive. Rule abstentions count as wrong in all-rows accuracy. Incumbent rows score Hive's own decision (labels.system, and the artifacts LLM where present) against the same labels. `vs gold (C∩N)` is the Claude∩Neon gold subset.
